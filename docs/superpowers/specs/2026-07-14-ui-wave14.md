## Background

Chinese-meeting transcripts are dense walls of text. Even after LLM
postprocess (Wave 12) adds punctuation, scanning 30 minutes of transcript
to find "the budget number" or "the deadline date" is painful.

Goal of PR-44a: lightweight keyword / pattern highlighting in the
transcript view. Pure frontend (no LLM, no backend) for instant feedback.

## Goals

- Auto-highlight common categories:
  - Numbers (Arabic, Chinese 数字)
  - Dates / times (date patterns, `YYYY-MM-DD`, "明天", "下周三")
  - URLs and emails
  - English proper nouns (consecutive capitalized words)
  - User-supplied custom keywords (from postprocess hotwords, reused)
- Light / dark mode compatible colors
- Toggle in settings
- 4 locale i18n

## Non-Goals

- LLM-based keyword extraction (PR-44b)
- Click-to-jump (PR-44c, separate PR)
- Full-text search (PR-44b)

## Design

```
[raw text] -> split into tokens -> each token gets a category tag
                -> renderer wraps tokens in <mark class="hl-{category}">
```

Categories (CSS-driven):
- hl-number:    monetary / quantity emphasis
- hl-date:      dates and times
- hl-url:       URLs / emails (rare in meetings but useful)
- hl-proper:    English proper nouns
- hl-custom:    user-supplied keywords

## Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Regex catastrophic backtracking | Use linear-scan tokenizer, not single mega-regex |
| Wrong highlights (over-eager) | Categories are conservative; user can toggle |
| Chinese punctuation splitting | Tokenizer is character-class aware |
